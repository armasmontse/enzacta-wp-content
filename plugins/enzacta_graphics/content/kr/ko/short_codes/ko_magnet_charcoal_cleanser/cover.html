The Fermentation Artisan's Secret

The story of Galactomyces traces back to traditional fermentation artisans, whose hands were said to remain clear and supple even after years of work showed on their faces. This contrast drew attention to the naturally fermented yeast they handled every day and inspired interest in Galactomyces Ferment Filtrate.

Derived through natural fermentation, this skin-conditioning ingredient helps refine the appearance of rough texture and supports a clearer, smoother, more balanced-looking complexion.

사용 방법

HOW TO USE

STEP

01

Begin on Freshly Cleansed Skin

Immediately after cleansing, dispense a small amount into your palm. Applying it directly with your hands rather than a cotton pad allows you to fully experience the BOOSTER’s cushiony texture and skin-hugging feel.

STEP

02

Smooth & Press

Gently smooth over the entire face using soft, circular motions. Finish by wrapping your face with the warmth of your palms and pressing gently to help the formula settle comfortably into the skin.

STEP

03

Layer for Extra Care

When skin feels particularly rough or dry, apply two to three thin layers. This concentrated layering technique leaves skin feeling more hydrated, supple, and comfortably cushioned.
